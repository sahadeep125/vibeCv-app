Resume Builder for Students With No Experience

Not having a job history yet doesn't mean you have nothing to put on a resume. VibeCV helps students turn coursework, projects, internships, and extracurriculars into a professional, ATS-friendly resume, no prior work experience required.

What to Put on a Resume With No Work Experience

Recruiters hiring for entry-level and internship roles don't expect a job history, they're evaluating potential. Strong substitutes include relevant coursework and academic projects, internships or part-time work even if unrelated, volunteer work and extracurricular leadership roles, and technical or language skills and certifications.

Turning a Class Project Into a Resume Bullet

The skill most students lack isn't experience, it's translation, knowing how to describe a group project or assignment the way a resume bullet point should read: what you did, what tools or skills you used, and what the outcome was. VibeCV's AI does this translation automatically from a short description you provide.

Why ATS Checking Matters Even More for Students

Entry-level postings often get the highest volume of applicants, which means ATS filtering is aggressive. A student resume with no experience has even less margin for formatting mistakes or missing keywords, VibeCV's free ATS Score Checker is built to catch this before you apply.
