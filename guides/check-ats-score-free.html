How to Check Your Resume's ATS Score for Free

Your resume's ATS score tells you whether an Applicant Tracking System will let a human ever see it. VibeCV's built-in ATS Score Checker scans your resume for formatting issues, missing keywords, and structural problems in under a minute, completely free.

What Is an ATS Score, Exactly?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is the software that most large companies use to filter incoming resumes before a recruiter ever opens them. Your ATS score is a percentage-based estimate of how well your resume will parse, match keywords, and pass that first automated filter.

Scores below roughly 50% usually mean sections are being misread, key skills are missing, or formatting like tables, columns, or images is confusing the parser.

Why Checking It Matters Before You Apply

Most job seekers only find out their resume failed the ATS after weeks of silence. Checking your score before you hit apply turns a guessing game into a fixable checklist: you see the exact gaps instead of wondering why you never heard back.

What VibeCV's ATS Checker Actually Scans

  • Format check — flags tables, text boxes, and layouts that ATS software can't parse correctly.
  • Structure scan — confirms your resume has the sections recruiters and bots expect, like Summary, Experience, Skills, and Education.
  • Keyword match rate — compares your resume's language against the skills and terms real job postings use.

Quick Answers

Is VibeCV's ATS score checker really free?

Yes. Checking your resume's ATS score is free in VibeCV, with a daily check limit on the free plan and more checks per day on the Pro plan.

What's considered a good ATS score?

Aim for 80% or higher. Scores in the 50 to 79% range usually pass basic parsing but are missing keywords; anything below 50% risks being filtered out before a recruiter sees it.

Does a high ATS score guarantee an interview?

No, it guarantees your resume reaches a human. After that, the quality of your experience and how well it matches the role still matters, which is why VibeCV also gives you a one-tap AI fix for weak sections.
